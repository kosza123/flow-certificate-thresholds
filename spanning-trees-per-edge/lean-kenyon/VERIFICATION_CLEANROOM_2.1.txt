Manual reading of Kenyon.numTrees_K4:

  numTrees (![0, 0, 0, 1, 1, 2] : Fin 6 → Fin 4) (![1, 2, 3, 2, 3, 3] : Fin 6 → Fin 4) = 16

i.e. edge i joins src i and tgt i: 0-1, 0-2, 0-3, 1-2, 1-3, 2-3, the six edges of K4 on
vertices 0..3, each once; 16 = 4^2 agrees with Cayley's formula. numTrees counts edge sets T
with |T| + 1 = |V| that connect every pair of vertices. The proof reduces IsTree to
"|T| = 3 and three reachability rounds from 0 reach every vertex" (using exists_crossing)
and counts the 64 edge sets with plain `decide` (kernel reduction). It uses no sorry, no
native_decide and no `decide +kernel`.

6. LIMITATIONS
--------------

* The compiled Mathlib files were taken from the official Mathlib cache; Mathlib itself was
  not rebuilt from source and was not replayed in the kernel here. leanchecker replayed the
  29 modules of the package.
* The toolchain archive was taken from the official GitHub release; its checksum was not
  compared with an independent source.
* #print axioms reports the axioms used by the listed theorems; it says nothing about
  whether the statements express the intended mathematics. Only numTrees_K4 was read
  manually here; the other statements were not re-read in this run.
* The verification was done by an AI session, not by a human.
