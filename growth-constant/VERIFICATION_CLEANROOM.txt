7. Remarks and limitations
--------------------------
(a) verify_gadgets.py must be started from its own directory.  Started from the repository root
    (python3 growth-constant/verify/verify_gadgets.py) it stops with
        FileNotFoundError: [Errno 2] No such file or directory: 'gadgets.json'
    after the H7 checks, because gadgets.json is opened relative to the current directory.
    The script was not changed.
(b) The Lean toolchain (elan, Lean 4.37.0-rc1) was already installed in the container; it was
    not downloaded again.  Mathlib, its dependencies and the Mathlib cache were downloaded anew.
(c) The run used the same container image as the development of the package, not a second
    machine or operating system.
(d) Building the parent package (PlanarCore) on its own in the same clone was not repeated here;
    it would download its own copy of Mathlib into repo/.lake/packages.
(e) The paper (tex/pdf) was not rebuilt.  Its numerical claims were checked only as far as
    verify_gadgets.py checks them.
(f) What the Lean package does NOT prove is listed in the header of TwoPort/Main.lean: planarity
    and co-faciality of the iterated networks (hypothesis hbridge), the hypotheses of the Fekete
    step, and the reading of the matrix entries as spanning-tree counts.
